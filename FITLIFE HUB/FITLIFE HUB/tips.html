Fitness Tips

Healthy living is easier when habits are simple, realistic, and consistent.

Start with realistic goals

Set goals that match your schedule. For example, working out three times per week is better than planning seven workouts and quitting after one week.

Warm up and cool down

A short warm-up prepares your body for exercise. A cool-down helps your breathing and heart rate return to normal.

Use good form

Proper form helps prevent injuries and makes exercises more effective. Start with lighter movements before increasing difficulty.

Balance fitness and rest

Rest days are part of progress. Sleep, hydration, and recovery are just as important as the workout itself.
